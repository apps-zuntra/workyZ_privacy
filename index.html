Workzi Privacy Policy

What personal data Workzi collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

Effective: 26 November 2026 • Last updated: 26 November 2026 • Operator: Zuntra Digital Private Limited • Governing law: India
This Privacy Policy explains how Zuntra Digital Private Limited ("Zuntra", "we", "us" or "our"), the company that owns and operates Workzi, processes personal data when you use the Workzi mobile apps for iPhone and Android, the Workzi web app and our websites (together, the "Services"). Our apps are published under the name Appszuntra.

Workzi is a workforce attendance and team-management platform used by organisations to record attendance with location-verified check-in, manage leave, shifts, tasks and announcements, and keep their teams connected.

This Policy is published under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and is written to meet the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("DPDP Rules") as their provisions come into force. Please read it together with our Terms of Service.

We do not sell personal data. We do not show advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Location is read only while the app is open, when you check in or check out. 2 Our Role and Your Employer's Role

Workzi is a business service. Organisations (the "Customer", usually your employer) create a Workzi workspace and invite their people to use it. This affects who is responsible for your data:

2.1 Workforce Data. For attendance, leave, shift, task, announcement, team feed and employee-profile data inside a Customer's workspace, the Customer is the Data Fiduciary: it decides why and how that data is used. Zuntra acts as the Customer's Data Processor and processes that data only to provide the Services and on the Customer's documented instructions.
2.2 Our own data. For sign-in and account security, customer support, safety and content moderation, billing, website visits, and service improvement, Zuntra is the Data Fiduciary.
2.3 What this means for you. Your employer's own policies (for example on attendance, location verification and HR records) apply in addition to this Policy, and your employer is responsible for them. If you contact us about Workforce Data, we will help you and, where required, pass your request to your employer and support them in responding.

3 Key Terms

3.1 "Personal data" means any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to that data.
3.2 "Data Principal" or "you" means the individual the personal data relates to, including employees, managers, HR staff and administrators who use Workzi.
3.3 "Data Fiduciary" means the person who decides the purpose and means of processing personal data; "Data Processor" means a person who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
3.4 "Administrator" means a person the Customer has authorised to manage its workspace, such as an owner, HR user or manager.
3.5 "User Content" means posts, comments, announcements, task notes, request reasons and other content that users enter into Workzi.

6 Legal Grounds and Consent

6.1 Consent. Where we rely on your consent, we ask for it clearly, for a specified purpose, and you may withdraw it at any time as easily as you gave it, by changing the setting in the app or on your device or by writing to us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that already took place.
6.2 Employment purposes. Your employer may process Workforce Data for the purposes of employment, which is a legitimate use permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act, or on another ground that applies to it. Your employer is responsible for giving you any notice it is required to give and for choosing the correct ground.
6.3 Sensitive information. Passwords, and any health-related information such as blood group, are treated as sensitive personal data under the SPDI Rules, 2011. Optional fields are only collected when you choose to fill them in, and by doing so you consent to their use for your employer's HR and emergency records.
6.4 Aadhaar. Entering an Aadhaar number is optional and is never required to use Workzi. Workzi does not perform Aadhaar authentication or e-KYC and does not verify the number with UIDAI. If you enter it, it is visible only to you and your organisation's authorised Administrators. We recommend that you enter only what your employer requires.
6.5 Legal obligations and other grounds. We may also process personal data where required to comply with law or a court order, to respond to a medical emergency or threat to safety, or for any other legitimate use permitted by law.

7 Location Data

📍
Only at check-in and check-out, only while the app is open
Workzi asks for "While Using the App" location access. It reads your location only when you check in or check out, and it does not track you in the background, between check-ins, or after work.
7.1 When you check in or check out, the app compares your device's location with the office area your employer configured, and looks up an approximate street address using your phone's mapping service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android).
7.2 We store whether you were inside the office area (office or remote work mode) and the approximate address. Exact GPS coordinates are used on your device for this calculation and are not saved to your attendance record.
7.3 Maps inside the app are provided by Google Maps. When a map is shown, Google receives the information needed to display it, under Google's own privacy policy.
7.4 You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working, but location-verified check-in will not be available, and your employer may ask you to use another method.
7.5 Location results are visible only to you and to authorised managers and Administrators of your own organisation.

8 Team Feed and User Content

8.1 Posts, comments and announcements are visible only to members of your own organisation. They are never shown to other Customers or to the public.
8.2 Before content is published, it is automatically checked against a list of objectionable words. Content that fails the check is not posted.
8.3 Any member can report a post or comment, or block another member, from the ••• menu. A reported post is hidden for the reporter immediately. Reports are sent to our moderation team, who review them and act on them within 24 hours, which may include removing content and suspending or removing the user who posted it.
8.4 Blocking hides the blocked member's posts and comments from you. Your block list is stored with your account and you can unblock members at any time.

12 How We Protect Personal Data

12.1 We use reasonable security practices in line with the SPDI Rules, 2011 and the DPDP Rules, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, role-based access rules that keep each organisation's data separate and restrict what each role can see, hashed passwords, access limited to personnel who need it, and monitoring for misuse.
12.2 No system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us immediately if you think your account has been misused.
12.3 Personal data breach. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users without delay and in the manner required by law, explaining what happened, the likely consequences, what we are doing and what you can do. We will also report the breach to the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In as required. Where the breach concerns Workforce Data, we will promptly notify the Customer and support it in meeting its own obligations.

14 Deleting Your Account

14.1 You can delete your Workzi account at any time in the app from More → Delete Account, or by emailing support@appszuntra.com from your registered email address.
14.2 When you delete your account, your sign-in is removed immediately and you are signed out. Your remaining profile data, push tokens, block list and other personal data are deleted within 30 days.
14.3 Some records may be kept after deletion where the law requires it or your employer must keep them, for example attendance and leave records your employer needs for labour or tax law, and the records described in Section 11. These are kept only for that purpose and then deleted.
14.4 If your employer closes your access, your employer decides what happens to your Workforce Data. You may still ask us to delete the personal data for which we are the Data Fiduciary.

15 App Permissions and Your Choices

  • Location ("While Using the App"): for check-in and check-out only. You can change it in your device settings.
  • Photos: only when you choose a profile picture. Only the photo you pick is uploaded.
  • Notifications: for reminders, approvals and announcements. You can turn them off in your device settings or in the app's notification settings.
  • Optional profile fields: you can leave them blank, or clear them at any time.
